=== Where Winds Meet GvG Layout & Rules (Comprehensive) ===

Title: Advanced 30v30 GvG Strategic Layout & Field Rules
Purpose: To coordinate a 30-player guild roster into distinct operational units, maximizing map economy, managing lane pressure, and securing the main capture objective.
Size: 30v30

=========================================
1. PRIMARY & SECONDARY OBJECTIVES
=========================================

Main Objective:
  Shatter the enemy's structural defenses (Outer and Inner Towers), breach their main headquarters, defeat the enemy faction boss (the Goose), and successfully execute an organized carrier escort to bring the Fortune Tree/Flag completely back to our home base line.

Sub-Objectives (Map Economy & Buff Control):
  * The Jungle & Camp Farming: Clearing active neutral monster camps across the wilderness sectors to harvest "Fun Coins" (WWM Jungle Money).
  * World Boss Contested Hunts: High-health neutral bosses that drop massive team-wide combat buffs (such as flat damage increases, health regeneration, or cooldown reduction) when secured.
  * Neutral Capture Points (Altars/Shrines): Capturing and holding small static positions on the map that grant passive Fun Coin generation over time or unlock fast-travel/respawn forward tents.
  * Defensive Outposts: Fortified choke points that slow down the enemy's main ball. They must be defended or sieged using specialized tactical purchases.

=========================================
2. THE TACTICAL ECONOMY (FUN COINS)
=========================================
* Teams start with 0 fun coins at start of game. 
* Accumulation: Fun Coins are gathered primarily by Flex teams clearing camps, defeating World Bosses, or keeping your defensive positions alive. Once a defensive position is destroyed, there are no more passive fun coins
* Expenditure: Coins are spent dynamically by the Shotcaller/Commander to deploy:
  - Battleground Commander Weapons: Boom chickens can be used to defeat jungle enemies. Horskies can be used as a temporary quick mount. Ballista bolts to have one way teleports anywhere on the field. 
  - Commander Board Skills: Battlefield-wide tactical abilities such as enemy healing reductions, allied respawn increases, and fortification invlunerability.

=========================================
3. ROSTER DIVISION & EXPECTED OUTCOMES
=========================================

--- Flex #1 (Roaming, Economy, & Skirmishing) ---
* Size: 5-8 Players (High mobility, strong 1v1/small-scale duelists, assassins)
* Core Role: Active Map Control & Jungle Dominance.
* Strategic Focus:
  - Rapidly clear jungle creeps to pool Fun Coins into the guild bank.
  - Constantly scout and contest neutral World Bosses before the enemy can anchor them.
  - Execute sudden flanking maneuvers onto the enemy Main Ball's backline squishies (healers/ranged DPS).
  - Apply split-push pressure on side lanes to force the enemy to break up their main force.
* Expected Outcome: Out-farming the enemy economy, securing a majority of team buffs, and disrupting enemy rotations.

--- Flex #2 (Defensive Anchor & Scouting) ---
* Size: 4-6 Players (High survivability, heavy crowd control, durable builds)
* Core Role: Base Defense, Choke Point Retention, and Tactical Intel.
* Strategic Focus:
  - Maintain an absolute lock on our home base perimeter and protect our Goose from stealth or backend split-pushers.
  - Anchor the main defensive lane/tower line, using heavy crowd control to stall out massive enemy pushes.
  - Act as the "eyes of the map." Since they sit in a stable defensive position, they track enemy movements across lane boundaries and relay scouting calls to Flex #1 for targeted counter-attacks.
* Expected Outcome: Preventing backdoor losses, minimizing tower structural damage, and giving the team map-wide vision.

--- Main Ball (Core Offensive Force) ---
* Size: 15-20 Players (Standard frontline tanks, heavy AoE DPS, dedicated group healers)
* Core Role: Main Lane Push, Forced Group Engagements, and Structural Siege.
* Strategic Focus:
  - Group tightly in the primary conflict lane to clash directly with the enemy's main force.
  - Protect and advance alongside deployed siege weapons to systematically smash down enemy outposts and towers.
  - Push inside the enemy base once structures fall to burn down the enemy faction boss (the Goose).
* Expected Outcome: Forcing the enemy into unfavorable large-scale team fights, shattering their structural defenses, and clearing a path to the final boss.

--- Carrier Group (Sub-Unit Inside Main Ball) ---
* Size: 3-5 Specific Players (Ultra-tanky characters, high movement speed, invulnerability/shield skills)
* Core Role: Flag Capture & End-Game Escort.
* Strategic Focus:
  - The moment the enemy Goose falls, a designated runner immediately claims the Fortune Tree/Flag.
  - The surrounding escort group burns heavy defensive resources—such as Nameless Sword shield walls to block incoming projectiles, or Lion's Roar silences to disrupt enemy crowd control—to build a protective bubble around the flag bearer.
* Expected Outcome: Safely escorting the Fortune Tree/Flag through high-pressure choke points and back across the map to secure the final match victory.

=========================================
4. SKILLS, BUFFS, & PURCHASABLES
=========================================

--- Tactical Purchasables (Fun Coin Economy) ---
*   Ballista Bolts
    - Cost: 5,000 Fun Coins (Pack of 12)
    - Strategic Use: High-velocity projectiles used to traverse and fly around the map rapidly, giving teams immense rotational speed and map-wide presence.
*   Horskies
    - Cost: 500 Fun Coins
    - Strategic Use: Deployed as temporary ground mounts to quickly push down a lane. Best used to disorient the enemy during skirmishes and maneuver swiftly across ground objectives.
*   Boom Chickens
    - Cost: 200 Fun Coins per chicken
    - Strategic Use: High-impact explosive assets best used for engaging dense enemy clusters of larger than 8 players, particularly when contesting the neutral World Boss or clearing large jungle sectors.

--- Commander Board Skills & Strategic Buffs ---
*   Morale Buff
    - Mechanics: Upgrades up to 8 distinct levels. Each incremental level requires a Fun Coin investment and triggers a cooldown of a few minutes.
    - Expected Outcome: Provides a stacking 5% damage and defense increase to all players per level, culminating in a massive 40% stat amplification once all 8 levels are achieved.
*   Hair Pulling
    - Mechanics: Boosts the entire team’s damage profile against the enemy faction boss (the Goose) by 100% for a brief 10-second window.
    - Expected Outcome: The designated burst window for the Main Ball. Shotcallers must coordinate and time high-damage weapon EX skills to land precisely during this active duration.
*   Sprint & Relentless Advance Combo
    - Mechanics: Sprint increases the Fortune Tree Carrier’s movement speed by 100% for 10 seconds. Relentless Advance grants the carrier interception immunity for 5 seconds.
    - Expected Outcome: The primary escape engine for the Carrier Group. Activating this combo allows the flag bearer to completely ignore enemy crowd control and break out of the high-pressure enemy base, making lane transit much easier once clear.
*   Last Stand
    - Mechanics: Dynamically reduces the team's respawn timers by 30% for 1 minute. If the guild falls behind by two or more towers, the recovery reduction automatically scales up to 60%.
    - Expected Outcome: A critical late-game stabilization mechanic used to mitigate long respawn penalties, prevent total map wipes, and sustain defensive lines under heavy siege.
*   Bounty Strike
    - Mechanics: Amplifies your team's direct damage output against the enemy Fortune Tree Carrier by 40% for 10 seconds.
    - Expected Outcome: The primary shutdown tool for defensive units. Flex teams or defensive anchors activate this to melt the enemy flag bearer and stall out their extraction.
*   You Got a Problem?
    - Mechanics: Increases the entire team’s overall structural damage against enemy towers by 100% for 10 seconds.
    - Expected Outcome: Used exclusively during coordinated siege pushes when the Main Ball is fully grouped, stacked with siege weapons, and ready to focus fire down a tower.
*   City Protection
    - Mechanics: Creates an absolute defensive barrier, preventing 100% of incoming damage to your guild's towers and Goose for 20 seconds.
    - Expected Outcome: A powerful defensive panic-button used to completely nullify a heavily committed offensive push from the enemy Main Ball.
*   Refight
    - Mechanics: A high-cost, premium skill that instantly slashes remaining weapon EX skill cooldowns by 50% while amplifying overall damage and healing outputs by 10% for 30 seconds.
    - Expected Outcome: The ultimate game-defining utility. Reserved strictly for decisive, large-scale team fights where a synchronized second wave of EX skills can completely turn the tide of battle.
